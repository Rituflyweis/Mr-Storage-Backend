## 3. Personal information we collect

### 3.1 Identifiers and contact data

We may collect:

- first and last name;
- email address;
- phone number and country code;
- company name;
- mailing or job-site location / city / state / ZIP;
- customer ID or account identifiers we assign.

**Sources:** you (chat, forms, portal profile), sales staff entering a lead, public records you provide, and files you upload.

### 3.2 Account and security data

- password (**stored hashed**, not in plain text);
- password-changed timestamps;
- reset OTP (stored temporarily; expires, typically within about ten (10) minutes);
- profile photo URL if you upload one;
- role (for staff: admin, sales, plant, construction, accounts, etc.);
- whether an account is active or deactivated;
- session tokens (including JWT access/refresh tokens) used to keep you logged in.

### 3.3 Commercial and project data

- lead and project names, job IDs, building type, dimensions, loads, colors, materials;
- estimates, quotations, version numbers, approval status, approval history, cover messages;
- invoices, line items, tax amounts, payment schedules, PO numbers, due dates;
- payment proofs (receipt files, transaction ID, payment date, amount, notes, review status);
- delivery, plant, BOM, freight-bid, and vendor-quote information tied to a job;
- notes, follow-up dates, and call scripts our staff use internally.

### 3.4 Communications

- chat messages and form free-text;
- emails we send or receive (including To / CC on quotations and invoices);
- SMS content we send for reminders or drop-off follow-up;
- enquiry notifications sent internally to [info@steelbuildingdepot.com](mailto:info@steelbuildingdepot.com).

### 3.5 Files and drawings

Drawings, photos (including invoice line-item images), PDFs, BOMs, shipper files, payment receipts, and similar documents. These are typically stored with cloud object storage (Amazon S3 or equivalent).

### 3.6 Vendor and carrier data

Business name, submitted file name and URL, quoted amount, notes, and submission history when a tokenized public link is used.

### 3.7 Technical and usage data

IP address, timestamps, browser/device type, pages or APIs requested, error logs, and similar diagnostics. We use this to operate, secure, and debug the Services.

### 3.8 Information we do not intentionally collect

We do not require Social Security numbers through public chat. Do not send sensitive government ID numbers in chat unless we specifically request them through a secure process. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18 (see Section 12).

## 4. How we collect information

- **Directly from you** — chat, forms, portal, email, phone, SMS replies, file uploads.
- **Automatically** — cookies/tokens, logs, and analytics needed to run the app.
- **From our staff** — when a salesperson or admin creates or updates a customer, lead, quote, or invoice.

We do not buy marketing lists as a core practice of these Services. If that changes, we will update this Policy.

## 5. Why we use personal information (purposes)

We use personal information to:

1. **Respond to enquiries** and operate chat, including matching or creating a customer and lead.
2. **Quote and sell** pre-engineered metal buildings, materials, doors, and related services: estimates, quotations, contracts, drawings, and internal **approval workflows** (submit, approve, reject, versioning).
3. **Invoice and collect payment**, including sending invoices, tracking due dates, reviewing uploaded payment proofs, and marking invoices paid or rejected.
4. **Perform the project** — engineering, plant, construction, deliveries, freight bids, vendor files, and customer-portal status.
5. **Communicate** — quotes and invoices (To/CC), OTPs, follow-ups by email/SMS, and notices to [info@steelbuildingdepot.com](mailto:info@steelbuildingdepot.com).
6. **Secure the Services** — authentication, deactivating staff, audit logs, fraud and abuse prevention.
7. **Comply with law** — tax, accounting, legal claims, and responding to lawful requests.
8. **Improve operations** — quoting accuracy, support quality, and reliability (including limited use of AI tools to summarize quotes or draft follow-up language, using project context you already provided).

We do **not sell** personal information as that term is commonly used in U.S. state privacy laws, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 10. Retention

We retain information as follows (unless a longer period is required by law or a dispute):

| Data | Typical retention |
|---|---|
| Customer, lead, quote, invoice, payment, and project files | Life of the relationship plus a period needed for warranty, tax, and legal records (often several years after project close) |
| Chat and enquiry content | As long as the lead/customer record is retained |
| OTP codes | Until expiry or use (about 10 minutes) plus a short security log |
| Audit / activity logs | As needed for security and operations |
| Staff accounts | Duration of authorization, then as required for employment and security records |
| Vendor upload tokens and submissions | Duration of the bid/job plus a reasonable archive period |

You may request deletion (Section 13). We may keep information we must retain for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention reasons, in which case we will limit further use.

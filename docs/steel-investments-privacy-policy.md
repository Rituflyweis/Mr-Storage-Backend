If you give us your mobile number, we may send **service texts** (OTP, quotes, invoices, project updates).

**Marketing / promotional SMS** are sent **only if you separately opt in** (for example an optional unchecked checkbox). Consent to marketing SMS is **not required** to buy from us.

When you opt in to promotional texts:

- We store your number and opt-in time.
- **We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.**

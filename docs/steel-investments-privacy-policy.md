## What we collect

Information you give us (name, email, phone, company, location, messages, files, payment receipts) and information we create (customer/lead/project records, quotes, invoices, logs). We also collect basic technical data (IP, browser, timestamps) to run and secure the Services. Passwords are stored hashed.

---

## How we use it

To respond to enquiries; provide quotes, invoices, and project services; communicate by email or SMS; verify accounts (including OTP); process payments and review receipts; operate staff workflows; comply with law; and protect against fraud or abuse.

We **do not sell** personal information. We **do not share** it for third-party cross-context advertising.

## Who we share with

Only as needed: Steel staff (role-based access); hosting and file storage (e.g. AWS); email delivery (e.g. SendGrid); **SMS delivery (e.g. Twilio)**; vendors/carriers on your project; advisers; authorities when required; or a successor in a business transaction. Providers use data only to perform services for us.

## Email and SMS

We may send **transactional** messages (OTP, quotes, invoices, project updates) to contact details you provide.

**Marketing SMS** only if you **separately opt in** (e.g. optional unchecked box on a form). **Message and data rates may apply.** Reply **STOP** to cancel marketing texts, or email [info@steelbuildingdepot.com](mailto:info@steelbuildingdepot.com). Consent to marketing SMS is not required to buy from us.

**SMS opt-in data:** When you opt in to promotional texts from Steel Building Depot, we store your number and opt-in time. **We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.** Numbers are shared only with our SMS provider to deliver messages you agreed to receive.

See [Terms of Use — SMS](./steel-investments-terms-of-use.md#sms-text-messaging) for program details.
